## 中文

### 這週做成了什麼

這次我把一個原模型 MLP 區塊的前向與反向，和固定參考逐項核對。指定案例通過原先門檻，讓後續接線有可以檢查的起點。它使用真權重轉成 FP32，加上人工輸入；範圍仍是一個區塊。

我也把先前分散的研究整理成較完整的公開目錄，補上已查過的歷史正負結果與近期資料。公開的是成果、實驗證據和小型示範，完整核心程式維持私有。

### 沒做成的

接成多層後，14 個新種子案例全部沒有通過固定 FP32 門檻。局部元件的通過，還不能推成整個模型通過。部分負控制也未過，修補另存，原失敗保留。

正式 BF16、完整模型、True 更新保存接續與新能力收益沒有新增通過證據。

### 接下來

- 先處理多層數值偏差與負控制，再按原門檻檢驗。
- 繼續整理成果與證據，將來源、實跑與能力結論分開說明。

## English

### What we got done

I compared one original-model MLP block's forward and explicit backward calculations against a fixed reference. The specified case met the unchanged tolerance, using original weights cast to FP32 and artificial inputs. The scope remains one block.

I also organized a broader public research catalogue, historical positive and negative results, and recent evidence. Complete core implementations remain private.

### What didn't work

All 14 fresh-seed multilayer synthetic CPU cases failed the fixed FP32 gate. Local component agreement does not establish full-model agreement. Some negative controls failed; the fix is separate and original failures are preserved.

There is no new accepted result for production BF16, the complete model, formal True update/save/continuation or capability gains.

### What's next

- Address numerical differences and controls, then test against the unchanged gate.
- Publish suitable results and evidence with clear source, execution and capability boundaries.

## Technical record

### Completed this week

- October 13: original layer-0 MLP weights cast to FP32, artificial inputs and cotangents, CUDA forward and explicit VJP.
- October 14: 14 fresh-seed synthetic CPU cases, 4/8 layers, hidden64/intermediate128/vocab97.
- October 15: catalogue and evidence publication work, without a model rerun.

### Validation and figures

- MLP: 151,027,712 compared elements; maximum absolute error about 3.72529e-9.
- Multilayer: 0/14 passed; 1,776 out-of-bound elements; worst error/bound about 39.65448.
- G raw uint8 equivalence: 14/14. Shared negative controls: 104/112; complete protocol failed.
- The new example checks evidence and arithmetic only.

### Failures and unfinished work

- Numerical root cause remains unproven; tolerance was not relaxed.
- The control fix is separate from unchanged frozen v3.
- Native BF16, full layers/32-layer execution, formal True integration and capability gains remain unaccepted.

## 2026-10-18 增補

A100 上的 9B 等級模型完成兩次主機指定短文字的更新，範圍為 47 組選定參數。第一次有 6 組參數改變；新程序精確恢復第一次保存狀態後，再更新 9 組參數，達 step 與 revision 2，並保存不同且確認持久保存的 checkpoint。

第二個 checkpoint 尚未由第三個程序再次載入。原逾時與恢復指標錯誤仍保留為失敗。這次結果沒有驗證自主提案、任意自由輸入、全參數訓練、一般數值等價、效能加速或能力提升。

10/14 的 FP32 0/14 與負控制 104/112 仍是原契約下的失敗結果。[10/18 公開摘要](../evidence/20261018_a100_python_cuda/summary.public.json)僅發布白名單數字、限制及私有原件雜湊承諾；完整核心、權重、保存點與原始張量維持私有。本次整理沒有重跑模型。

## October 18, 2026 addendum

A 9B-class model on A100 completed two updates for 47 selected parameter tensors using host-specified short text. Six parameter tensors changed on the first update. A new process exactly restored the first saved state, completed a second update that changed nine parameter tensors, reached step and revision 2, and saved a different checkpoint with native durability confirmation.

The second checkpoint has not been loaded by a third process. The original timeout and initial recovery-pointer failure remain failures. This case does not establish model-led proposals, arbitrary freeform input, all-parameter training, general numerical equivalence, speedup, or capability gains.

The October 14 FP32 0/14 and negative-control 104/112 results remain unchanged under their original contract. The [public summary](../evidence/20261018_a100_python_cuda/summary.public.json) contains allowlisted observations, limits, and hash commitments to withheld records. Complete cores, weights, checkpoints and raw tensors remain private. Preparing this summary did not rerun the model, and the public summary alone cannot reproduce the private model workflow.
